# The minimal board

The smallest useful Smash&Clash client on a tldraw board, about 140 lines in `script/main.js`. It plays the house opponent with plain shapes: click **New game**, click a card in your hand, then a tile. Effects and hops appear as buttons named by their move (`FLIP`, `BOULDER(D2)`, `hop→E3`).

To try it: open any board in tldraw Desktop, choose **Develop → Reveal Script…**, and copy both files from `script/` into the folder it opens.
